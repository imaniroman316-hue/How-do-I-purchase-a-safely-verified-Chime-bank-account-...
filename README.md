2. The Agitation: Hidden Risks Behind Ready-Made Accounts
The main concern with purchasing an existing account is the lack of control over its history and ownership. A seller might promise full access, but the original account holder may still have information needed to recover the account.
Imagine paying for an account and using it to receive an important payment. A few days later, the account requests additional identity verification. If the account details belong to someone else, you may not be able to complete the process.
The situation can become more difficult if money is already in the account. You could face delays while trying to establish ownership or resolve an account restriction. You might also have difficulty recovering the money paid to the seller.
Another concern is personal information. Some sellers may request identity documents, passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive details. Sharing these details with an unknown person can expose you to identity theft or unauthorized transactions.
Claims such as “100% verified,” “lifetime access,” and “guaranteed security” should not be treated as proof. No seller's promise can guarantee that a financial institution will accept a change of control or maintain access to an account.
